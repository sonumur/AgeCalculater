How Our Online Calculators Work — The Science Behind the Tools

Every number you see on a Pepstudio result screen comes from a well-established mathematical or scientific formula. We did not guess, round incorrectly, or cut corners. Here is a plain-English look at what is happening under the hood for each tool:

Age Calculator

Uses Gregorian calendar subtraction — subtracting the day, month, and year of your birth date from today's date, then borrowing correctly from months that have 28, 29, 30, or 31 days. Leap years (divisible by 4, with century corrections) are handled precisely so February 29th birthdays are never miscounted.

BMI Calculator

Applies the World Health Organization formula: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For imperial users, the equivalent is [weight (lbs) × 703] ÷ height (in)². Results are classified into WHO categories — Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25–29.9), and Obese (30+).

EMI Calculator

Uses the standard amortization formula: EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the loan tenure in months. The same formula banks use.

Percentage Calculator

Handles three common percentage problems: finding X% of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, and calculating percentage increase or decrease. All three formulas are standard arithmetic — no approximations.

Unit Converter & World Time

Unit conversions use fixed, internationally recognised conversion factors (e.g., 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly). World Time uses the browser's built-in Intl API with IANA timezone data to show accurate local times worldwide — including daylight saving adjustments.
