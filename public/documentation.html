Pepstudio Technical Documentation & System Guide

A comprehensive guide to our precision calculation engine, mathematical models, client-side privacy architecture, and utility suite functionality.

memory 1. System Architecture & Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Every tool across the pepstudio suite is engineered with a client-first paradigm. Unlike legacy web converters and financial utility sites that transmit form inputs to remote backend servers, pepstudio performs 100% of computational logic inside your local browser runtime.

When you input your date of birth, loan principal, or personal metrics, no HTTP request contains your data. This architecture delivers two fundamental benefits:

  • Zero Latency: Output calculations are rendered instantly upon keypress, bound only by client CPU evaluation speed.
  • Cryptographic Privacy: Confidential financial figures and health indicators never leave your device.

functions 2. Calculation Models & Mathematical Specifications

🎂 Chronological Age & Anniversary Algorithm

Our age calculation algorithm calculates exact elapsed time between birth date $D_1$ and target date $D_2$, accounting for variable month lengths (28 to 31 days) and Gregorian leap year adjustments ($Y \bmod 4 = 0 \land (Y \bmod 100 \neq 0 \lor Y \bmod 400 = 0)$).

Age(Y, M, D) = ElapsedYears(D_1, D_2) + ElapsedMonths(D_1, D_2) + ElapsedDays(D_1, D_2)

⚖️ Body Mass Index (BMI) & WHO Classification

Body Mass Index evaluates height-to-weight proportions according to World Health Organization (WHO) standards:

BMI = \frac{\text{weight (kg)}}{\text{height (m)}^2}

Categories: Underweight (< 18.5), Normal (18.5 – 24.9), Overweight (25 – 29.9), Obese (≥ 30).

🏦 Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Formula

Loan payments utilize the standard actuarial monthly compounding annuity model:

EMI = P \times r \times \frac{(1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}

Where $P$ is principal loan amount, $r$ is monthly interest rate ($\text{annual rate} / 12 / 100$), and $n$ is total months ($\text{tenure in years} \times 12$).

📐 Unit Conversion & Arbitrary-Precision Scaling

Unit conversions rely on standardized SI base unit conversion matrices (e.g. converting length through meters as intermediate standard), eliminating floating-point rounding inaccuracies.

🌐 World Time & IANA Time Zone Synchronization

World Time computations utilize the `Intl.DateTimeFormat` engine synced with official IANA Time Zone Database specifications, automatically adjusting for Daylight Saving Time (DST) transitions globally.

wifi_off 3. Offline Mode & PWA Capabilities

Pepstudio web tools are fully compatible with Progressive Web App (PWA) caching standards. Once you load a tool page, script logic is cached in your browser's local cache storage, enabling full offline operation when internet connectivity is unavailable.
