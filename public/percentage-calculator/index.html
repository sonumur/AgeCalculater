Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate percentage increase between two numbers?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

What is the formula for calculating X% of a number?

Multiply the percentage number X by the total value Y, then divide the result by 100: (X × Y) / 100.

Why are percentages useful in finance and daily life?

Percentages standardize comparisons across different scales, making it easy to analyze interest rates, shopping discounts, tax rates, and statistical trends.

Is X% of Y equal to Y% of X?

Yes! Because multiplication is commutative, X% of Y is mathematically identical to Y% of X (e.g., 8% of 50 = 4, and 50% of 8 = 4).

How do I calculate a discount percentage during a sale?

To find your savings on a discounted item, multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. For example, a 20% discount on a $60 item is (60 × 20) / 100 = $12 saved, making the final price $48.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease between two values?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the result by the original value, and multiply by 100: [(New − Original) / Original] × 100. A positive result indicates a percentage increase; a negative result indicates a decrease.

Fun Facts & Math Trivia

Etymology of Percent

The word "percent" comes from the Latin per centum, which literally translates to "by the hundred".

Mental Math Reversibility

To calculate 16% of 25 in your head, flip it to 25% of 16 (one quarter of 16), which equals 4 instantly!

Ancient Roman Taxes

Ancient Romans levied taxes in fractions of 100 (like 1/100 on auction sales) centuries before the % symbol existed.

Financial Basis Points

In stock trading, 1% is divided into 100 "basis points" (bps), so a 0.25% interest rate hike equals 25 basis points!
