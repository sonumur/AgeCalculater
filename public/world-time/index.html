What is a World Time Clock & How Does It Work?

Welcome to the Pepstudio World Time Clock—a dynamic, real-time global clock and timezone converter designed for international business, remote teams, travelers, and global families! Tracking time across different continents and time zones can be confusing due to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) offsets, Daylight Saving Time (DST) changes, and international date line boundaries. Our interactive World Time dashboard displays live local time alongside a curated grid of major global financial capitals including London, New York, Tokyo, Paris, Sydney, and Dubai.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between UTC and GMT?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the atomic time standard used worldwide, while GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is a time zone based on the prime meridian. In practice, both share the exact same time.

How does this World Clock handle Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

Our clock automatically updates for Daylight Saving Time changes in real-time based on official IANA timezone database rules for each individual city.

How do I compare time zones for cross-border meetings?

Use our "Compare Offset" slider to shift time forward or backward by hours. All city cards will instantly update to show what time it will be in each location.

What is the International Date Line?

The International Date Line is an imaginary line located near 180° longitude. Crossing it westwards adds 24 hours (one day forward), while crossing eastwards subtracts 24 hours (one day back).

How many official time zones exist in the world?

There are 38 officially recognised time zones worldwide when counting half-hour and quarter-hour offsets. Standard integer UTC offsets range from UTC-12 to UTC+14, but fractional offsets like UTC+5:30 (India) and UTC+5:45 (Nepal) add to the total count.

Why does India use UTC+5:30 instead of a whole-hour offset?

India uses UTC+5:30 (Indian Standard Time) as a compromise to keep the country under one single national timezone despite spanning over 29° of longitude. The 30-minute offset was originally set during British colonial administration to split the difference between the eastern and western extremes of the subcontinent.

Fun Facts & World Time Trivia

First Nation in New Year

The island nation of Kiribati sits in UTC+14, making it the very first country on Earth to welcome the New Year!

45-Minute Time Zones

Nepal uses UTC+5:45—one of only three places in the world with a 45-minute fractional timezone offset!

China's Single Time Zone

Despite spanning 5 geographical time zones, China officially operates on one single nationwide timezone: Beijing Time (UTC+8).

Space Station Time

Astronauts aboard the International Space Station see 16 sunrises a day and operate strictly on UTC time!
